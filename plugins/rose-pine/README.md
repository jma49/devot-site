# Rosé Pine

Soho vibes in muted rose, pine and gold: Dawn for Light Mode; Rosé Pine and Moon for Dark Mode.

Themes: Rosé Pine Dawn (Light Mode), Rosé Pine (Dark Mode), Rosé Pine Moon (Dark Mode).
Choose one in Settings > Appearance, for Light Mode or for Dark Mode.

Every color of Devot is drawn from the Rosé Pine palette by Rosé Pine (https://github.com/rose-pine/rose-pine-theme), under the MIT License in `LICENSE`.
The theme files are generated by `pnpm plugin-themes` from `scripts/plugin-themes/palettes.ts`.

Needs Devot 1.1.8 or later.
