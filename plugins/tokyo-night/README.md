# Tokyo Night

The blues and violets of a city at night: Tokyo Night Light for Light Mode; Tokyo Night and Storm for Dark Mode.

Themes: Tokyo Night Light (Light Mode), Tokyo Night (Dark Mode), Tokyo Night Storm (Dark Mode).
Choose one in Settings > Appearance, for Light Mode or for Dark Mode.

Every color of Devot is drawn from the Tokyo Night palette by Enkia (https://github.com/enkia/tokyo-night-vscode-theme), under the MIT License in `LICENSE`.
The theme files are generated by `pnpm plugin-themes` from `scripts/plugin-themes/palettes.ts`.

Needs Devot 1.1.8 or later.
